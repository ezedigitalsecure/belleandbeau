Good to know
01How does reserving work?+
Choose what you'd like and send the form below. No payment is taken now. We'll email you to confirm your order, the total and how you'd like to pay, then let you know when it's ready.
02Collection & delivery+
Collect from our studio in Calne at your next appointment, or ask us about UK delivery when you reserve.
03Ingredients & allergens+
Full ingredient lists are printed on every label. Our products contain essential oils and nut oils (sweet almond, shea and cocoa butter). If you have a nut allergy or sensitive skin, please ask us before ordering.
04Using your products safely+
Oils and balms are for external use only. Patch test before first use and avoid the eyes. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding or under medical care, check with your GP first. Calm Sanctuary and the car diffusers are concentrated blends for fragrance use only. Never apply it to the skin, and keep it away from children and pets.
