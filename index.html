Natural shape refinement
Natural shape refinement.

We create fully personalised non-surgical plans to smooth, firm and balance your natural form — targeting pockets and contours that diet and exercise alone have not shifted.

Working across tummy, waist, hips, thighs, arms, back and chin, our core approach uses laser lipolysis, cavitation and radio-frequency to gently reduce stubborn fullness, lift and tighten skin, improve elasticity, and soften the look of stretch marks and scarring.

To build on these results we can combine care with Manual Lymph Drainage, massage, gua sha, cupping, wood therapy, and carefully selected injectables — all woven together to suit your goals and deliver a look that feels like you.

How we work

Considered, not rushed.

Belle & Beau is an inclusive aesthetics practice built around comfort as much as results, for every body.

We keep our client list intentionally small — so every appointment gets full, unhurried attention, honest guidance, and never a rushed slot. We take time to understand what matters to you, explain all options clearly, and only move forward when you feel fully confident.

We use only clinically-graded equipment maintained to the highest standards. For all injectable treatments we work closely with regulated prescribers where required. Before any procedure you will have a clear plan, know exactly what to expect, and receive full, easy-to-follow aftercare guidance. All personal information is handled securely in line with GDPR and UK data-protection rules.
