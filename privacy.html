Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy matters to me. This policy explains what personal information this website collects, why, how long it is kept and the rights you have under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

1. Who we are

This website is run by Belle & Beau Aesthetic Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17402375). We are the data controller for the personal information collected through this website. Treatments take place at The Hideaway, 8 Phelps Parade, Calne.

If you have any questions about your information, email hello@belleandbeauaesthetic.com.

2. What we collect and why

Our website forms do not ask for health information, so please do not include medical details in a form or review. Health questions are covered during your private consultation.

3. Our legal reasons for using your information

  • Consent: for marketing emails, the Glow Ritual emails and publishing reviews. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: to reply to enquiries, manage reservations and run the business safely.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep records.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We only use trusted services that help run the website:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) passes what you type into our forms to our email inbox.
  • Our email provider stores the emails we send and receive.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log basic technical information such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on this site, so your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Fresha handles treatment bookings for The Hideaway. When you book, Fresha's own privacy policy applies.

Some of these services may store information outside the UK. Where they do, they are required to protect it to UK standards.

5. How long we keep it

  • Email list and Glow Ritual details: until you unsubscribe.
  • Reviews: for as long as the review is published, or 2 years if it is not.
  • Reservations and enquiries: up to 2 years after we last speak.

6. Cookies

This website does not set any cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. If we ever add them, we will ask for your permission first and update this policy.

7. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. Every marketing email includes a way to unsubscribe. To use any of these rights, email hello@belleandbeauaesthetic.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
